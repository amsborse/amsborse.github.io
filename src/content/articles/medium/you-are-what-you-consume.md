## 🌌 **The Cosmic Joke We Call Free Will**

If your body is made of food, and your mind is made of what you see,<br>then what are _you_ really, if not a mixed playlist of what you consume?

Your bones are built from breakfast.<br>Your worldview? Imported daily via phones.<br>You’re not a mystery — you’re a reflection.<br>And yet we act surprised when our lives feel off-track,<br>as if we didn’t build the track with every bite and every scroll.

Your body rebuilds itself every seven years. Your mind rebuilds itself every seven seconds. You are not a statue — you’re a bonfire, fed by what you throw into it.

Eat trash, feel trash. Watch trash, think trash.

## 👁️‍🗨️ **The Horror of Mindless Inputs**

Imagine this: You’re trying to write a novel using only junk mail and expired coupons.<br>That’s what most people are doing with their future.<br>You dream of clarity, but you feed on chaos.<br>You crave purpose, but you snack on passivity.

The real horror isn’t that you’re stuck — <br>It’s that you keep forgetting **you’re the one holding the remote.**

And today, you flipped the channel.<br>You’re here, reading this, not drifting through reels.<br>That one tiny pivot? It already bent your timeline.

## 💪 **The Audacity of Choosing Better**

Here’s the bold move no one claps for:<br>Choosing things that makes you a _more stronger version of you._

A five-minute article instead of five-second dopamine.<br>A nourishing meal instead of a sugar spell.<br>A curious question instead of a complaint.

These aren’t just habits.<br>They’re <em>declarations.</em><br>Each one quietly says:<br><strong>“I’m building a mind I want to live inside.”</strong>

## ✨ **The Loom of Destiny Is Made of What You Notice**

Here’s the thing they don’t tell you about fate: it’s not some mystical script written in the stars. It’s the sum of every piece of information you absorb, every thought you feed, every habit you repeat.

Your brain is a prediction machine. It takes what you feed it — news, conversations, books, memes — and says, _“Ah, so this is the world?”_ Then it builds your future actions based on that data. Watch enough political rage-bait? Your brain assumes the world is hostile. Read stories of human ingenuity? It starts spotting opportunities instead of obstacles.

And the only words you’ll ever know<br>are the ones you let into your head.<br>Your future is composed of your vocabulary.<br>Your imagination borrows from your inputs.<br>You will only dream as far as your diet allows.

What you consume becomes your raw material for creation — <br>your decisions, your direction, your default reactions.

Destiny isn’t fate.<br>It’s just _pattern recognition_, built from everything you’ve absorbed.

So if you want to change where you’re going,<br>start by changing what you’re made of.

Your destiny isn’t handed to you — it’s _assembled_ from the raw materials you consume.

## 🧘 **Peace Is the Side Effect of Conscious Consumption**

You don’t have to fight your destiny.<br>You just have to feed it better ingredients.

Your body becomes your actions.<br>Your mind becomes your questions.<br>And your destiny becomes whatever _those two_ decide to build.

So eat like your future depends on it.<br>Watch like your soul’s under construction.<br>Scroll like you’re curating your karma.

Because you are.

Your life is a feedback loop.<br>What you consume is what you become — <br>and what you become is what shapes what you consume next.

Stars died so you could choose your breakfast. The universe conspired to give you this body, this mind, this moment. The least you can do is _feed them like you give a damn._

So start the loop somewhere new.<br>Somewhere bold.<br>Somewhere quiet enough…<br>for destiny to find you thinking.
