And then God got bored, decided to play the ultimate multiplayer game, shattered into infinite tiny pieces, and now we’re all here — arguing over WiFi speeds, pineapple on pizza, and the meaning of life. 🎭 😂. The truth is, we were not born with any instruction manual. This freedom to define our own purpose also traps us, as we too often confuse what is truly important with what merely seems urgent.

## The Trap We All Fall Into

In the grand scheme of things, how much of what we concern ourselves with on a daily basis truly matters? We find ourselves consumed by deadlines, responsibilities, and societal expectations — the endless stream of “important” tasks that demand our attention. Yet, when we pause to reflect, an unsettling question arises: why do these seemingly crucial priorities often feel so utterly meaningless? This paradox lies at the heart of the modern human experience. We are perpetually chasing what the world deems important, only to find that the things that fill our calendars and to-do lists rarely align with what makes our lives feel truly meaningful. Unraveling this disconnect is essential if we hope to live with intention and purpose. By understanding the distinction between importance and meaning, we unlock the power to shape our own experience — to choose, with intention, where we invest our precious time and energy.

The daily grind of commuting, sitting in meetings, and meeting deadlines may feel incredibly important in the moment — these are the tasks that keep us employed and provide financial stability. However, the true meaning may lie in the relationships we build with colleagues, the sense of purpose we derive from contributing to a larger mission, or the intellectual stimulation we get from problem-solving. The important tasks are the means, but the meaningful experiences are the ends.

That’s the paradox: **importance and meaning aren’t the same thing.** Understanding the difference could be the most liberating realization of your life. Get this wrong, and you might spend years optimizing for urgency while neglecting purpose.

## ⚫Importance vs. Meaning: What’s the Difference?

**Importance** is external — it’s what society, your job, or even your calendar tells you matters. It’s urgent, pressing, and often tied to consequences. Miss a mortgage payment? Important. Submit that project by Friday? Important. Answer emails within 30 seconds? Also, somehow, important.

**Meaning**, on the other hand, is deeply internal. It’s what makes life worth living. It’s why people leave six-figure jobs to paint, travel, or teach. It’s why you remember a random late-night conversation from ten years ago but forget what you had for lunch yesterday. **Meaning doesn’t scream for attention; it just exists, waiting for you to notice it.**

### ⦿The Liberating Truth

You don’t have to abandon importance — but don’t let it dictate your life. Prioritize meaning, and you’ll find that the truly important things will align naturally. **The trick is knowing when to stop chasing what _seems_ urgent and start embracing what _is_ fulfilling.**

Because at the end of the day, you won’t remember the emails you sent, the meetings you attended, or the tasks you checked off. **You’ll remember the things that had meaning.** Choose wisely.

* * *

## ⚫The Power of Choice: Shaping Importance and Meaning

Many people don’t realize how much power they have over their own perspectives. We have the ability to choose **to what extent** we attach meaning and importance to things. We can decide **which activities** deserve our deep emotional investment and which are simply necessary tasks. Most importantly, we can also choose **whether we want to attach meaning or importance at all.**

Why is this crucial? Because without conscious choice, we default to societal programming — chasing priorities that may not align with our true values. When we recognize that meaning and importance are within our control, we gain:

- **Freedom from external pressure:** You don’t have to care about something just because the world tells you to.
- **Intentional living:** You get to decide what truly matters to you and invest in that deeply.
- **Emotional balance:** You stop wasting energy on things that don’t contribute to your personal fulfillment.

By being intentional about what we value and to what extent, we take control of our own fulfillment. And that’s one of the most powerful choices we can make.

### ⦿ **A Journey Beyond Comfort Zones**

Have you ever noticed how your mind tends to fall back on familiar choices, even when the stakes are higher? It’s almost like a built-in mechanism, guiding you towards what feels safe, even when you know you could do more. This is not laziness or indecision — it’s your mind’s energy-saving strategy. In its most primal form, your mind is designed to keep you alive, and to do that, it conserves energy. Making decisions takes energy, and the act of evaluating your options isn’t just a passive process. It requires _discernment_, which is a costly operation, both mentally and emotionally.

So, why does it seem like our brains prefer the easy route, even when it leads to less growth or excitement? Over time, as we make more and more choices, our minds develop a tendency to fall back on the “safe” options we’ve already established. By safe I mean whatever choices you made for importance and meaning before. The reason? It’s simply easier. In the moment, evaluating fresh choices feels like a drain on energy, and who wants to deal with the mental exhaustion when the past choice already worked well enough? This is the essence of the comfort zone — the place where our minds feel at ease because we’ve done the mental legwork already. The result? We opt for what we know, even when there are more exciting possibilities just outside our reach.

The real meaning of stepping out of your comfort zone is learning how to feel comfortable in the discomfort itself. It is the unwavering trust in yourself that you can handle whatever life throws at you. It’s about feeling alive, tapping into the excitement of the unknown without the fear of making mistakes or losing control. This isn’t about forcing yourself into a negative situation but finding joy in the journey of discovery.

### ⦿ Choose With Intention

How do we do this? The answer lies in the _act of choice_. When you actively choose where to put your attention, you are living intentionally. Whether it’s learning a new instrument, reading a book, or attending a meditation retreat, every choice you make adds meaning to your life. It’s the simple act of deciding that this moment, this experience, matters. You don’t have to push yourself into discomfort for the sake of change — you simply have to choose with intention.

Of course, one could argue that completely disregarding importance in favor of pure meaning is not a practical approach to life. After all, we all have financial obligations, social expectations, and responsibilities that require us to attend to the pressing and urgent. The key is to find the right balance — to engage with the important tasks, but not let them consume us to the point where we neglect the truly meaningful aspects of our lives. It’s a nuanced dance, and different people will strike that balance in different ways depending on their circumstances and priorities.

Exercising the power of choice begins with self-reflection. Take some time to consider what truly brings you joy, fulfillment, and a sense of purpose. What activities or experiences make you feel most alive and engaged? These are the things you should be prioritizing, even if they don’t align with societal definitions of importance. Then, start making intentional choices to carve out time and space for these meaningful pursuits. It may mean saying no to certain obligations, or rearranging your schedule to create more room for what matters most to you. Small, incremental choices add up, and over time you’ll find that your life becomes increasingly aligned with your deepest values and sources of meaning.

**Imagine a life where every day is filled with intentional choices.** What would it feel like to spend your time on things that truly excite you, rather than just moving through the motions? The more you make conscious choices, the more your life transforms into a journey of growth and fulfillment.

So, take a moment to reflect. Have the courage to explore, to step beyond your comfort zone, and to see what draws others to those forest retreats or hours of deep learning. The universe is always evolving — embrace the journey and evolve with it.

Ultimately, the power of choice is in your hands. It’s not about making _all_ the right choices, but about choosing with purpose and embracing the adventure that follows. By doing so, you allow yourself to evolve, to discover new dimensions of yourself, and to live a life that feels meaningful. Wouldn’t it be better if every day felt like an exciting opportunity to grow and evolve?

## “I Have to Do This” → “I Want to Do This”

Shift your perspective from **“have to”** to **“want to.”** This simple change rewires your brain, easing resistance and making tasks feel lighter. The words you choose shape your experience — speak to yourself with intention.

Even with something as routine as exercise, saying **“I want to exercise”** instead of **“I have to”** transforms the experience. It’s not just about language; it’s about reclaiming choice. Apply this shift to everything you do, and watch how your mindset — and your life — begins to change.

## ⚫The Art of Creating Meaning

Lately, I’ve been drawn to sunrises and sunsets—not because they’re inherently important (they are if sun just suddenly disappeared all life on earth will cease to exist in few weeks), but because _I_ make them meaningful.

Think about it. The sun rises and sets every day, whether we notice it or not. It doesn’t demand attention, it doesn’t fight for significance. And yet, the moment I _choose_ to stand there, watching the colors shift from deep blues to fiery oranges, feeling the warmth spread over my skin, sensing the crisp air before dawn—it becomes something more.

<blockquote class="medium-pullquote"><p><strong>Meaning isn’t something you find. It’s something you bring.</strong></p></blockquote>

Most of us drift through moments lost in thought, replaying the past or strategizing the future. But meaning doesn’t exist in a distracted mind. If you’re stuck in your head, the moment never becomes real—it just passes, unnoticed, unclaimed. But when you’re fully present, engaged in the now, that moment transforms. It stops being _just another_ sunrise and starts being _your_ sunrise.

And that’s the secret: **any moment can be meaningful if you decide to be fully in it.** It’s not about the sunrise, the coffee, the conversation, or the task—it’s about _you_ showing up for it.

### ⦿ [Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience](https://g.co/kgs/ajqxfoh) By [Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi](https://g.co/kgs/DY5TZhy)

Ever been so immersed in something that time seemed to disappear? That’s **flow state** — a mental zone where action and awareness merge, and nothing else exists except the task at hand. It’s in this state, not in passive observation, that we experience the deepest sense of meaning.

Most of us go through life distracted, half-present, bouncing between notifications and unfinished thoughts. But meaning isn’t found in scattered moments — it’s created in **full immersion.** Whether it’s playing music, writing, coding, or even an intense workout, flow pulls you in so completely that your mind stops overanalyzing, and you simply _exist_ in the doing.

And here’s something wild — **you’re not just “you” in this moment.** Your body is a community of billions of single-celled organisms working in perfect synchrony, each responding to your focus. When you enter flow, it’s not just your mind aligning with the task — your entire biological system is engaged, operating at peak efficiency. **Your body, mind, and existence merge into one seamless experience.**

Flow state isn’t just about getting things done — it’s about experiencing life in its most **pure, unfiltered, and meaningful form.** The more you cultivate it, the more life stops feeling like something happening _to you_ and starts feeling like something _you are creating it._

### ⦿ [Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End](https://g.co/kgs/4qn9SrW) — An Anime Suggestion

**If the journey is over, what comes next?** [_Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End_](https://g.co/kgs/JcPrRJS) explores the quiet, profound question of finding meaning after purpose fades. The Demon Lord is defeated, the hero’s quest is complete — yet for Frieren, an elf who lives for millennia, time stretches endlessly ahead. As she watches her human companions age and pass, the series unfolds into a deeply moving meditation on time, impermanence, and what truly gives life meaning. Thought-provoking and beautifully told, _Frieren_ isn’t just a story — it’s an invitation to reflect on how we value our own fleeting moments.

## ⚫Closing Thoughts

As I sit here, reflecting on the interplay between importance and meaning, I can’t help but wonder: Why do we often confuse the two?

You see, importance is the urgent pressure we feel from the outside world — deadlines, expectations, the things that we _must_ prioritize to keep up with the flow of life. It feels real, because it’s tangible, and it often dictates our daily choices. But meaning? Meaning is the quiet force that lives inside, the guiding star that ensures we’re on the right path. It’s the deep, authentic reason why we do anything at all.

The trick is to learn how to separate the noise of what is _important_ from the calm of what is _meaningful_. Because, let’s face it — life can feel like an endless race toward things that _matter_ in the moment, but if we’re not careful, we may miss the things that _truly matter_ in the grander scheme.

So, as we wrap up today’s exploration, I leave you with this: **Don’t let the world define your importance. Instead, carve out space to discover your meaning.** And when you do, you’ll find that everything you thought was important falls into place — no longer as a burden, but as part of a bigger, more fulfilling story.
