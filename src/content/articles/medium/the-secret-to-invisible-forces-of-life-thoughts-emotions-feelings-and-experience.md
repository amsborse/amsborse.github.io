Ah, the grand theater of the human experience — where thoughts reign supreme, emotions run rampant, and feelings are but the fickle whispers of an unruly body. Or so we’re led to believe, aren’t we?

You see, the true power doesn’t lie in the grand musings of the mind or the dramatic rollercoaster of your heart. No, the real magic happens when you learn to separate the signal from the noise, to distinguish the objective facts from the subjective stories we tell ourselves.

While thoughts are merely mental constructs, feelings are the raw, undeniable physical sensations happening within our bodies. Warmth, tightness, tingling — these are the real data points sent by our physiology. Emotions, on the other hand, are the psychological interpretations and stories we attach to these bodily sensations. Anger, joy, fear — these are just conceptual labels, not objective truths.

Blasphemy, you say? Heresy most foul? Well, prepare to have your world turned upside down, because in the sections that follow, we shall peel back the curtain on the invisible forces that have been puppeteering your existence. Because if there’s one thing we can all agree on, it’s that reality is merely a construct, and the true power lies in the fantasies we conjure up between our ears.

Strap in, this is going to be a wild ride through the rabbit hole of your own consciousness.

To understand all these human created words lets also understand another word which will help in laying foundation and concretizing some of the concepts ↠ “reality”.

## 1️⃣ Reality: The Truth You Choose to Believe

Reality isn’t as simple as it seems. It exists in two distinct forms:

🔹 **Existential Reality** — The objective truth, independent of beliefs (e.g., the sun, gravity, nature).<br>🔹 **Psychological Reality** — The world your mind constructs based on thoughts, beliefs, and conditioning (e.g., words, race or ethnicity, money).

### ➼Existential Reality: What Truly Exists

Existential reality is what is objectively true. It exists independent of human thoughts, opinions, or interpretations.

🔹 The **sun** exists. It doesn’t care what you think about it.<br>🔹 **Gravity** exists. It pulls you down whether you believe in it or not.<br>🔹 The **ocean, mountains, air, and sound** are all part of existential reality.

These are **nature’s creations**, existing long before human beings arrived and continuing long after we are gone.

But not everything we interact with is part of existential reality. Some things are human-made concepts — and that’s where psychological reality comes in.

### ➼ Psychological Reality: Personal Reality

Your personal reality is a psychological construct — **a world created in your mind based on your experiences**, conditioning, and beliefs.

Here’s an example:

**_➳ Words don’t exist in existential reality — only sound does. —_** Letters, language, and meanings are all human inventions. Your name? That’s not real either. It’s something society assigned to you. Words don’t exist in nature. What does exist? Sound.

Animals don’t need words to understand the world — they rely on instinct and direct experience. So do humans. But somewhere along the way, we started trusting language and thoughts over our own experience.

Think about it: If someone calls you **“stupid,”** what actually hurts you?

🔹 The **sound of the word** doesn’t harm you.<br>🔹 The **letters of the word** doesn’t attack you.<br>🔹 The pain comes from **your mind interpreting the word and giving it meaning**.

The world will always throw words, opinions, and narratives at you. **But none of them have power unless you give it to them.**

Here are some more examples that might help.

**_1. Money_ 💰**

- A piece of paper or digital number has no inherent value — it’s just an agreed-upon symbol of worth.
- If society stopped believing in money, it would instantly become meaningless.

**_2. Time_ ⏳**

- The universe doesn’t follow clocks or calendars — only humans do.
- “Minutes” and “hours” are measurements we created to structure life, but time itself is just a concept.

**_3. Laws & Borders_ 🌍**

- Nature doesn’t recognize countries or laws — they are imaginary lines drawn by humans.
- A bird flying across “borders” doesn’t get arrested — it simply moves through space.

**_4. Success & Failure_ 📈📉**

- There is no universal definition of success or failure — these ideas exist only in the human mind.
- A tree doesn’t “fail” to grow — it just adapts. The idea of success is purely a human construct.

**_5. Right & Wrong_ ⚖️**

- The universe doesn’t categorize things as “right” or “wrong” — only humans do. In the universe its only “cause” and “effect”.
- What’s considered “good” in one culture may be “wrong” in another.

**_6. Beauty & Ugliness_** 💄

- A flower doesn’t think it’s beautiful — humans project that onto it.
- Beauty standards change over time, proving they are subjective, not real.

## 2️⃣ Thoughts: The Illusion of the Mind

Your thoughts are not real — they are abstract interpretations shaped by past knowledge, memories, and learned concepts.

Your mind is a storyteller, constantly narrating reality through its own filter:

🔹 You can wonder about magic, but that doesn’t make it real.<br>🔹 You can imagine failure, but that doesn’t mean it will happen.<br>🔹 You can remember a past fight, but it’s not happening now.

Thoughts are limited — they can only recycle what you already know. That’s why you can’t “think” your way into a completely new reality.

Two people can experience the same event but react completely differently — not because of the event, but because of their thoughts about it. One sees failure as an ending, the other sees it as success. One thinks a rainy day is gloomy, another thinks it’s cozy.

Your thoughts create the first ripple in the chain. Get them wrong, and everything downstream collapses; get them right, and you build a reality where you thrive. The choice is yours: actively decide which thoughts will create a better ripple effect. **Your mind isn’t just a passive observer — it’s the architect of your experience.** So, start choosing thoughts that create the ripple effect you actually want.

Once you stop believing every thought, you free yourself from unnecessary suffering.

## 3️⃣ Feelings: The Only Thing That is Real

Unlike thoughts, **feelings are real**.

They are **physical sensations** happening in your body right now. They don’t require interpretation.

🔹 The warmth in your hands? Real.<br>🔹 The tightness in your chest? Real.<br>🔹 The tingling in your stomach? Real.

Feelings are **chemical and biological processes** — your body digesting food, new cells growing, nerves firing. They happen whether you think about them or not.

### ➼ **Feelings exist entirely within you.**

A breakup doesn’t create sadness — **your body does.** It’s simply a chemical response to loss. A compliment doesn’t bring you joy — **your brain releases dopamine** because it perceives validation.

But here’s the key:

## 4️⃣ Emotions: The Biggest Lie You’ve Been Told

What if I told you that emotions aren’t real? That they are just **feelings with a story attached to them**?

Society, books, and movies have taught you to label feelings as happiness, sadness, anger, love, fear, and guilt — but these are just concepts. Emotions are not real; They are psychological interpretations of physical sensations.

### ➼ Your Mind Creates the Story

The mind craves understanding — it can’t rest until it makes sense of things. This is why stepping outside your comfort zone is hard. Uncertainty feels threatening to the mind, so it fills in the blanks with a story to make things feel safe.

### ➼ ✅ Feelings ⟺ ❌ Emotions

Your body sends signals, but **how you interpret them** depends on the story you attach. For example:

✅<strong>Your heart beats fast:</strong><br>❌ **Fear** — If you’re in a hospital.<br>❌ **Anxiety** — If you’re in an interview.<br>❌ **Excitement** — If you’re with someone you like.

✅<strong>Your stomach feels heavy:</strong><br>❌ **Depressed** — If you’re feeling low.<br>❌ **Nervous** — Before a big decision.<br>❌ **Regret** — If you’ve made a mistake.

✅ <strong><em> Your chest feels heavy.</em></strong><br>❌ Are you sad? Overwhelmed? Emotional?<br>✅ <strong><em> Your body feels restless.</em></strong><br>❌Are you Nervous? Excited? Bored?<br>✅ **_You feel butterflies in your stomach._** <br>❌ Feel Love? Fear? Guilt?

Your mind will create a story depending on what is going on with your life and the situations around you.

## 5️⃣ Experience: Life Can Only be Experienced

Experience isn’t just what happens to you — it’s how you _perceive_ it. Think about it: your senses take in information, your mind assigns meaning, and your beliefs act as a filter, shaping reality before you even process it. If you believe words are reality, you’ll always be at the mercy of those who know how to trigger you. But here’s the truth: words, like thoughts, exist only in the mind — not in the physical world. They’re tools, not truths.

So, what else can you truly do other than experience life? Achievements, milestones, and accolades are just markers along the way — temporary stops that let you experience success. But the real transformation? It comes from what you _feel_, what you _learn_, and who you _become_ in the process.

No material thing will ever change you. Not the car you drive, the house you own, or the title on your business card. Only _experience_ has the power to transform you. Instead of chasing external validation, shift your focus to how you engage with life — your emotions, your thoughts, your interactions. These are the building blocks of your growth.

Experience shapes your truth and defines who you become. Every moment, every sensation, every emotion becomes a part of you. It’s in the way you laugh, the way you cry, the way you rise after a fall. That’s where real growth happens — not in the destination, but in the journey.

So, the next time you find yourself chasing an external goal, pause. Ask yourself: _How do I want to experience this moment?_ Because in the end, life isn’t about what you achieve — it’s about how you live, how you feel, and how you grow.

## 6️⃣ Reacting vs Acting

Have you ever yawned and immediately thought, <em>“I must be tired — I need sleep”</em>? That’s your mind **jumping to conclusions**, assuming that every feeling of yawn means exhaustion. But is that really true?

Yawning doesn’t always mean you’re tired. In fact, it can happen for many reasons:

🟢 **Low oxygen levels** in the brain.<br>🟢 **Cooling the brain** when it overheats.<br>🟢 **Signaling boredom** or lack of stimulation.<br>🟢 **A nervous response** in stressful situations.

So if you assume _“I must be sleepy”_ every time you yawn, you may misinterpret the situation and take the wrong action.

* * *

### 🚫Your Mind’s Default Mode: Automatic Reactions

Your brain loves shortcuts. It wants to **save energy**, so it creates **automatic reactions** based on what it has seen, learned, or been conditioned to believe.

🔴 You **yawn** → You assume you need sleep.<br>🔴 You **feel a racing heart** → You assume you’re anxious.<br>🔴You **get goosebumps** → You assume something is scary or emotional.

But here’s the truth: Just because your brain gives you a reaction doesn’t mean it’s correct. The biggest mistake people make is believing they know what to do based on automatic reactions. But reality isn’t that simple.

### 🚫Delusional Stories of Your Mind

Ever woken up feeling like **everything is wrong**? <br>🔴 <em>“I don’t want to go to work.”</em><br>🔴 <em>“I should’ve woken up earlier.”</em><br>🔴 <em>“I should’ve slept on time.”</em><br>🔴 _“Now I need to buy food, and it’s expensive and unhealthy.”_

Notice something

🟢 None of these thoughts are actually happening right now.<br>🟢 They are just predictions.<br>🟢 They are your brain’s attempt to explain why you feel the way you feel.

What’s actually happening?

🟢 Your body is experiencing unpleasant sensations / feelings.<br>🟢 Your mind, trying to make sense of these feelings, starts searching for external reasons.

The reality?

**Your body is just tired.** That’s it. No deeper meaning. No crisis. No existential disaster. I struggled with understanding this for quite some time but then I experimented I changed my diet and exercise habits and irrespective of the situation I was feeling good again. That’s all I needed to do, fix the physical body not the external stories.

### ✅ Transform Your Suffering Into Physical Pain

The next time you think you have a bad feeling just ask yourself “Could this have a purely physical cause?”

🟢 Maybe you didn’t get enough sleep.<br>🟢 Maybe you’re dehydrated.<br>🟢 Maybe you didn’t eat a proper meal yesterday.

### 🚫 Mind Creates Attributes

Most people decide whether they like or dislike something before they actually experience it. They let past experiences, opinions, or social conditioning decide for them.

🟢 But reality isn’t <strong>good or bad.</strong><br>🟢 It isn’t <strong>positive or negative.</strong><br>🟢 It isn’t **right or wrong.**

Reality just **is.**

When you call an experience _bad_, you’re not describing reality. You’re describing **your personal reaction to it.**

### ✅ Experience First, Judge Later

Here’s the truth:

🟢 You might have tried something in the past and disliked it, but maybe you were just <strong>tired, hungry, or distracted.</strong><br>🟢 You might have met someone and felt like they were annoying, but maybe you were just <strong>in a bad mood.</strong><br>🟢 You might think you hate a certain type of food, but maybe you just **never really tasted it with full awareness.**
