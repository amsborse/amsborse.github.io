Let me tell you about the day a rotting bird taught me how reality is made.

## 😢 When a Bird Becomes a Bullet

Somewhere in feudal Japan, an English sailor becomes the unlikely master of a house. In one scene from [_Shogun_](https://share.google/ymryYI8Fb5XLgTGlT), he jokes — half in jest, half in threat — that if anyone touches the rotting bird he plans to cook, they’ll be executed. One servant doesn’t understand. He moves the bird.<br>He’s killed.

And in that brutal silence, something shatters inside him.

The sailor rages, argues, pleads — “It was just a bird! Just a word!”

The lady replies, almost softly:<br><strong>“Yes, the bird was meaningless. But your words gave it meaning.”</strong>

## 👁️‍🗨️ The Lies We Live In

That line hit like lightning under my skin. Because I’ve done the same thing — not with birds, not with swords, but with thoughts.

Last year, my mind was a haunted house with bad plumbing. <br>“If there is no purpose Why work? Why achieve? Why grind?”<br>“If everything is destined where is the free will?”<br>“If we have free will why all the responsibilities to stop making free choices?”

At first, they felt true — not because they were, but because I kept turning them over in my hands like worn coins.

Here’s the secret no one tells you: Your brain is a meaning-making machine, not a truth detector. It will baptize any thought you linger on. Stay long enough in the cathedral of _I’m not enough_, and even the echoes start sounding like scripture.

But I said them enough times, and suddenly they _were_ facts. Not because they were true.<br>Because **my words gave them meaning**.

## 💪 The Spell of Self-Talk

We underestimate the spells we cast when we speak. To others and To ourselves.

Say “I’m lazy” every day and watch your spine soften.<br>Say “the world is broken” and notice how your eyes filter only cracks.<br>Words are not reflections. They’re blueprints. Every phrase you repeat chisels the walls of your inner home.

## ✨ Your Voice Is the Architect

You’re not trapped in your thoughts. You’re trapped in the **meaning** you’ve welded to them.

Thoughts come and go like clouds. But the moment you narrate them — “This is how I am” — you assign them power. You hire them as architects of your reality.<br>The mind isn’t a truth-teller. It’s a storyteller.<br>Be careful what genre you’re living in.

This isn’t “law of attraction” fluff. It’s neurology. Repeat _I’m terrible with money_ enough times, and your brain will skip over job listings, ignore savings tips, walk past opportunities — not because they don’t exist, but because you taught it what to filter out.

Gandhiji put it best: “Your beliefs become your thoughts. Your thoughts become your words. Your words become your actions. Your actions become your habits. Your habits become your values. Your values become your destiny.”

## 🌀 The Echo You Create in Others

You’re not the only one living in your words.

Sometimes we repeat things to others — harmless jokes, side comments, exaggerated truths — <br>“You’re always late.”<br>“You’re not cut out for this.”<br>“This is just how you are.”

And maybe we didn’t mean it. Maybe it was just a passing sentence.<br>But they started believing it.<br>Not because it was true.<br>**Because _you_ gave it meaning — and _they_ trusted your voice.**

You didn’t lie.<br>But you built a mirror they started seeing themselves through.

This isn’t about guilt. It’s about power.<br>Words are not just self-fulfilling.<br>They’re _other_-fulfilling too.

So speak gently.<br>Even in jest.<br>Because someone might be building a belief from your throwaway lines.

## 🧘 Speak As If It Matters

That servant in _Shōgun_ didn’t die because of a bird. He died because words — even careless ones — draw borders around what’s possible.

Your life right now is built on sentences you’ve been repeating since you were twelve. Some are yours. Most were handed to you. All can be rewritten.

So tonight, when the old whispers come, do one thing differently:

Leave them unanswered.

The most powerful meanings aren’t the ones we speak — but the ones we refuse to.
