## 🧩 Section 1: Energy Shapes Your Reality

_(How energy influences thoughts, memory, and imagination)_

Most people don’t need therapy. They need a nap. A good stretch. And maybe a bit of sunlight.<br>Because what they call a “problem” is often just a situation seen through the lens of **low energy**.

When you’re drained, even small things feel personal.<br>The comment feels like an attack. The delay feels like rejection. The plan feels impossible.

But when you’re full of energy?<br>Same situation, different you. You breathe and respond. You laugh it off. You get curious again.

> <em>“There are no problems in life. Only situations.<br>And whether they crush you or grow you depends on one thing:<br></em><strong><em>Do you have enough energy to meet them clearly?</em></strong><em>”</em>

### 1. It’s All Energy

Some mornings I’d wake up feeling optimistic, forgiving, focused.<br>Other nights, the same thoughts looked darker. I’d spiral. Doubt everything.

That’s when I realized — it wasn’t the thoughts that changed.<br>It was my **energy**.

> _“Your thoughts aren’t always wrong. Sometimes they’re just arriving to the wrong version of you.”_

When your system is aligned, you can respond. When it’s depleted, you react.

> _“Energy isn’t just fuel. It’s the lens through which you see life.”_

### 2. The Mind Obeys the Battery

Most people don’t realize this, but **your mind runs on the same battery as your body**.

When energy is high:

- You can focus
- You can direct imagination
- You can revisit memory with clarity

When energy is low:

- You spiral
- You catastrophize
- You remember pain, not perspective

> _“Your brain doesn’t serve you when you’re drained. It serves your survival.”_

Low energy activates your brain’s **threat response** (like the amygdala), which makes everything feel urgent, dramatic, unsafe.<br>So no — you’re not too sensitive.<br>You’re just **down a few bars**.

## 🔄 Section 2: How Your Past Hijacks the Present

_(Memory, emotion, and why you spiral even when nothing’s wrong)_

### 3. Memory & Imagination: Double-Edged Swords

When used consciously:

- Memory = reflection
- Imagination = vision

But when you’re tired?

- Memory = rumination
- Imagination = anxiety

Your body recognizes emotions **before** your mind does.<br>And when you feel a familiar discomfort, your body says:

> _“Ah yes, this is that same bad thing.”_

Your brain completes the pattern:

> _“See? I knew it. Life never changes.”_

And just like that — you’re not reliving the past.<br>You’re reliving the **feeling**.

### 4. How Your Body Remembers What Your Mind Forgets

Ever feel sore after an emotional day? That’s not random.

Your body stores tension.<br>It holds **unprocessed emotion**.<br>This is what some traditions call **karma** — not as mystical punishment, but emotional residue carried forward.

> _“Karma isn’t a cosmic report card. It’s your nervous system replaying unresolved loops.”_

Books like _The Body Keeps the Score_ show how emotion embeds in the body.<br>Stretching, breathwork, movement — they help **complete the loops** your mind keeps running.

## 🧘‍♂️ Section 3: How to Reset the System

_(Yoga, meditation, and emotional regulation)_

### 5. Why Yoga Actually Works

When you stretch in the morning, you’re not just loosening muscles.<br>You’re **resetting emotional patterns**.

Each movement tells your nervous system:

> _“That was yesterday. I’m here now.”_

Yoga isn’t about touching your toes.<br>It’s about not being stuck in your **storyline**.

> <em>You’re not just stretching your hamstrings.<br>You’re stretching your past.</em>

### 6. Meditation Is Not About Stopping Thought

If you’ve tried to “stop thinking,” you know how that ends.<br>The thoughts get louder. You get frustrated. You quit.

Here’s the truth:

> <em>Meditation isn’t about stopping thought.<br>It’s about</em> **_not following_** _every thought that shows up._

You sit. You breathe. You watch.<br>And then something subtle happens:<br>You stop being inside the thought.<br>You become the one watching it.

That’s power.<br>That’s peace.

Meditation tones the **vagus nerve**, calms the body, and trains the mind to stay **here**, instead of replaying the past or fearing the future.

* * *

### 7. Intuition: Clarity or Chemical?

Let’s be honest:

What most people call “intuition” is often emotional residue<br>plus a decision that happened to work out.

> _“Intuition isn’t always magic. Sometimes it’s just mood.”_

We rarely talk about the _wrong_ gut feelings.<br>Only the ones that confirm us.

Real intuition only arises when the system is **calm and centered**.<br>Otherwise, what you’re hearing isn’t wisdom.<br>It’s a whisper from your nervous system, still reacting to something old.

**🧘 Journaling Prompt: Reset the Pattern**

> _“I experienced \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ when \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, and it made me feel \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. I still think about it when \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.”_

Now reflect:

- What might I have misunderstood back then?
- What is my body still holding on to?
- What would a wiser version of me see now?
- What can I learn so I don’t repeat this?

This isn’t about fixing the past.<br>It’s about not bleeding it into your future.

* * *

## 🌅 Ask This Before You Spiral

Tonight, when your thoughts start spiraling, ask:

> <em>“Is this true?<br> Or am I just tired?”</em>

You don’t have to believe everything your mind says when you’re low.<br>You just need to recharge. Move. Breathe. Listen.<br>And ask better questions.

Now breathe.<br>Let today be new.
