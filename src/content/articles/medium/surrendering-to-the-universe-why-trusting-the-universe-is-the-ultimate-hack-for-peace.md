A friend ([@vegiverse](https://www.instagram.com/vegieverse?igsh=aWViMHRlNTBvMHho)) recently asked me, _“What’s your take on surrendering to the universe?”_ At first, I laughed. Not because the question was funny, but because it felt like the universe itself was nudging me to write about it. So here we are.

Let’s get one thing straight: It is not about blind faith in the universe rather it is having faith in your actions and your efforts.

## Why you should surrender?

Maybe you’re thinking, _I’m not sure this whole “trust the universe” thing really works._ **Not trusting it doesn’t give you any advantage — it only brings stress.** Think about it: how much time and energy do you waste worrying about outcomes? Playing out every possible scenario in your head, trying to predict the unpredictable, and stressing over things you can’t control? Now imagine redirecting that energy into something productive — like taking bold, inspired action toward your goals. That’s the power of trusting the universe. It’s not about being passive; it’s about being intentional with your energy and letting go of the need to control the outcomes.

## The Science Behind Surrendering to the Universe: Vibrations, Energy, and the Art of Letting Go

What if I told you that surrendering to the universe isn’t some woo-woo concept reserved for spiritual gurus, but a scientifically grounded practice that aligns you with the very fabric of reality? Let’s break it down — because when science and spirituality meet, magic happens.

Imagine zooming in on your body with a super-powered microscope. What would you see? A swirling dance of atoms and molecules, vibrating at incredible speeds. These vibrations aren’t just happening in your body — they’re the building blocks of the entire universe. Everything, from the stars in the sky to the chair you’re sitting on, is made up of energy vibrating at different frequencies.

Quantum physics tells us that at the subatomic level, particles exist in a state of potential until they’re observed. In other words, your attention — your focus — has the power to shape reality. When you surrender and align your vibration with your desires, you’re essentially observing them into existence.

Spiritual traditions have been talking about this for centuries, calling it by different names: [aura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aura_%28paranormal%29), [prāṇa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prana)([प्राण](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3)), [Qi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi) (chi). But at its core, it all points to the same truth: you are a vibrational being, and your frequency determines what you attract into your life. Think of it like a radio station. If you’re tuned to 98.5 FM, you’re not going to hear the song playing on 102.3 FM. Similarly, when you vibrate at the frequency of your desires, you align yourself with the energy of those desires — and the universe responds in kind.

### We are the Universe (Literally)

We were all born from the earth, we eat what grows from it, and when our time here is done, we return to it. In a very real sense, we _are_ the universe. The same energy that flows through the stars, the trees, and the oceans flows through us. So when we talk about surrendering to the universe, we’re really talking about aligning with the deepest part of ourselves.

## Attention: The Invisible Force That Shapes Your Reality

### Your Brain’s Personal Assistant: How the RAS Shapes Your Reality

Think of your attention as a lens. Whatever you focus on becomes clearer, sharper, and more vivid. Imagine you’re sitting in a crowded restaurant. There’s a chaotic mix of sounds — clinking plates, conversations from different tables, background music, and the occasional burst of laughter. Yet, despite all this noise, you can effortlessly tune in to the voice of the person sitting across from you while everything else fades into the background.

This happens because of your [**Reticular Activating System (RAS)** ](https://allenaustin.com/how-the-reticular-activating-system-ras-can-prevent-falling-into-the-confirmation-bias-trap-tpl-insights-246/)is a network of neurons located in your brainstem— your brain’s built-in filter. The RAS scans all incoming sensory data and decides what’s important for you to focus on. Since you _want_ to hear the person you’re speaking with, your brain amplifies their voice and minimizes everything else.

Now, imagine someone across the room suddenly says your name. Even though you weren’t consciously paying attention to their conversation, your RAS picks it up instantly. Why? Because your brain recognizes your name as _highly relevant_ information, even in a sea of noise.

This is how your RAS works every second of the day — not just with sound, but with thoughts, opportunities, and patterns. **Whatever you focus on, your brain will filter reality to highlight more of it.** Focus on problems? You’ll see more problems. Focus on solutions? Your brain will start filtering for those instead. The choice is yours.

Let’s explore some traps I’ve noticed in myself that were actively shaping my reality. Recognizing these helped me see how I was unknowingly holding myself back — and they might just help you identify ways you’re doing the same.

1. **The “Bad Day” Trap**: If you wake up thinking, _“Today is going to be a bad day,”_ your RAS will help you find evidence to support that belief. You’ll notice the long line at the coffee shop, the rude email from a coworker, and the traffic jam on your way home. But if you wake up thinking, _“Today is going to be amazing,”_ your RAS will highlight the kind stranger who held the door open, the unexpected compliment from your boss, and the beautiful sunset on your drive home.
2. **The “I’m Broke” Trap**: If you constantly tell yourself, _“I don’t have enough money,”_ your RAS will highlight every bill, every expense, and every financial setback. But if you shift your focus to abundance — _“I always have enough for what I need”_ — you’ll start noticing opportunities to save, earn, or invest.
3. **The “I’m So Unlucky” Trap**: If you constantly tell yourself, _“Nothing ever goes my way,”_ your RAS will help you find proof. You’ll notice every missed bus, every rainy day, and every minor inconvenience. But if you shift your focus to gratitude — _“I’m so lucky to have what I have”_ — you’ll start noticing small wins, like finding a parking spot or getting a discount at the store.

### The Universe Doesn’t Judge: What You Focus On Grows

The universe doesn’t care whether you’re focusing on problems or solutions. It doesn’t judge. Existentially, there’s no right or wrong — those are human inventions. The universe simply responds to your energy.

## The Nature Of Desire

Since we’re on the topic of desire, let’s take a moment to examine its true nature. What is desire, really? At its core, **desire is a contract you make with yourself to remain unhappy until it’s fulfilled.**

Everywhere you look, desires are being planted in your mind, whether you realize it or not. Society, family, friends, media — they’re all dropping seeds, hoping they take root. Everyone has an opinion on what should matter most. One person swears money is the key to freedom. Another tells you that nothing is more important than family. Someone else insists that inner peace through meditation is the ultimate goal😜. And guess what? They’re all right. But they’re not all _right for you_.

### Vague Desires, Vague Results: Why the Universe Needs a Clear Address?

The challenge isn’t in having desires — it’s in choosing which ones to foster. You can’t chase money, family, and meditation all at once with the same intensity. It’s like trying to drive in three directions at the same time. You’ll end up going nowhere.

The key is to pick _one_ desire at a time. Not because the others don’t matter, but because focus amplifies energy. Focus amplifies actions. Action gives results. When you pour your attention into one thing, you create momentum. You align yourself with the universe in a way that makes co-creation possible.

Think of it as setting your GPS. **You don’t need to know every turn along the way, but you do need to know your destination.**

### Picking a desire which aligns with your Internal Motivation

Why is internal motivation so rare? Because most people chase goals that were never truly _theirs_ to begin with.

Think about it — how often do you push yourself because of external rewards? Money, validation, social approval? And when those rewards disappear (or feel out of reach), so does your motivation. This is why so many people feel stuck, uninspired, and drained.

But **internal motivation is different**. It comes from within. It’s when you do something because it **aligns with your core values** — because **the act itself feels meaningful**, not just the outcome.

It’s the difference between running to burn calories vs. running because it makes you feel alive. Between writing for likes vs. writing because it help others and clarifies your thoughts. Between working for a paycheck vs. working because the work itself excites you.

The strongest motivation isn’t forced — it’s felt. Align your actions with what excites your soul, and you’ll never have to search for motivation again.

## Meditation: **The Art of Choosing Your Thoughts (Not Controlling Them)**

Most people think meditation is about stopping thoughts. It’s not. Thoughts will always come. But you _can_ choose whether to engage with them. **It’s about creating space between your thoughts and yourself, so you can observe without attachment.**

### Thoughts vs. Thinking: The Key Distinction

Here’s something most people never realize:

- **Thoughts** are automatic. They arise without your permission. **They pop up like notifications on your phone.**
- **Thinking** is a choice. It’s what happens when you latch onto a thought and start engaging with it. **To click on the notification is your choice.**

Imagine a stranger (thought) on the street yelling something unpleasant at you. Do you stop and argue with them? Or do you keep walking? You can’t stop a thought from appearing, but you _can_ decide whether to entertain it. What if you could simply notice the thought and say, <em>“Thanks, but no thanks”</em>? That’s the power of meditation. It trains you to step back, observe your thoughts, and decide which ones deserve your attention.

### Your Thoughts Are Neutral — It’s Your Perspective That Paints Them

**Thoughts don’t come labeled as positive or negative.** They show up as raw, neutral impulses. It’s _you_ who decides whether they’re good or bad, empowering or limiting, uplifting or destructive.

The thought _“I lost my job”_ could mean disaster, or it could mean an opportunity for reinvention. The thought _“I failed”_ could be proof that you’re not good enough, or it could be a stepping stone to something better. The thought _“I’m alone”_ could feel like isolation, or it could feel like freedom.

### The Freedom of Non-Engagement

Here’s the magic: when you stop engaging with thoughts, they lose their power. They might still show up — after all, thoughts are automatic — but they won’t stick around. It’s like turning down the volume on a noisy neighbor. Eventually, they get the hint and quiet down.

This doesn’t mean you’ll never have ̶n̶e̶g̶a̶t̶i̶v̶e̶ thoughts again. It just means you’ll have a choice. And that choice? It’s the root of all freedom.

## Faith Isn’t Blind — It’s Trust in Your Effort

People often misunderstand faith. They think it means sitting back, crossing their fingers, and hoping the universe magically delivers their dreams. That’s not faith — that’s wishful thinking. But real faith isn’t passive — it’s the deepest trust in your own effort, knowing that if you do your part, the universe will do the rest.

Imagine planting a seed. You water it, give it sunlight, and nurture it. But you don’t _force_ it to grow. You trust that with the right care, nature will take over. That’s faith. You do what’s in your control, then let go of the need to micromanage the outcome.

So, have faith — not in miracles without effort, but in your own persistence, and **in the truth that when you give your best, life has a way of giving back**.

### True Faith vs. Conditional Faith

Complete faith in the universe is a rare and elusive thing. Many people claim to have faith, but their actions tell a different story. Consider the practice that’s common in many cultures around the world. When people go to a temple or pray to God, they often ask for something in return — **“If you do this for me, I will give you my respect, my devotion, my offerings.”**

On the surface, it might seem like a normal act of faith. But at its core, it’s actually a form of **conditional faith**. It’s saying, _“I will believe in you, but only if you give me what I want.”_ This is not trust. This is a transaction.

When people operate from a place of conditional faith, they’re limiting themselves. They’re essentially saying, _“I’ll only believe in you if you behave exactly how I expect.”_ But the universe doesn’t work that way. It’s not a vending machine where you insert a prayer and get an outcome. It’s more dynamic, more subtle.

True faith isn’t about negotiating with the universe. It’s about surrendering without asking for a return.

### Letting Go of Doubt

Doubt isn’t just a mental hesitation — it’s a vibrational contradiction. You can’t fully align with your desires if a part of you is constantly questioning whether they’ll happen. Your energy becomes fragmented, like a radio signal full of static.

## Yoga: The Symbiosis Between You and the Cosmos

Yoga is often misunderstood as simply a physical practice — a series of stretches and poses to improve flexibility and strength. Yoga, at its core, is about symbiosis — a harmonious relationship between you and the cosmos. It’s about realizing that the boundaries between “you” and “the universe” are illusions.

## The Universe Handles the How, You Choose the What

Most people get stuck trying to figure out _how_ things will happen. They overanalyze, stress over the details, and hesitate to move forward because they don’t see a clear path. But here’s the truth: **the “how” is not your job**. Your only job? **Choose the “what.”** Because here’s the truth: you’re not here to figure out the how. You’re here to dream, to decide, and to delight in the magic of what happens next

Imagine placing an order at a restaurant. You don’t go into the kitchen and stress about _how_ the chef will prepare your meal. You just place your order, trust the process, and wait for it to arrive.

The same applies to life. When you obsess over the _how_, you block the natural flow. But when you clearly decide _what_ you want and take action in that direction, the universe aligns circumstances, opportunities, and people in ways you could never predict.

The universe is far more creative than your limited perspective. If you fixate on only one possible way things could work out, you might miss the infinite other ways they _could_.

So, set your intention. Be clear on your _what_. Then surrender the _how_.

The universe always finds a way — often better than you ever imagined.
