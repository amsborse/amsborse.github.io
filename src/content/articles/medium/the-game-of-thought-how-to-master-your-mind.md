## What If Your Mind Wasn’t Yours?

You wake up in the morning and start thinking.<br>But… _who taught your mind how to think?_

We assume our thoughts are original, our opinions our own.<br>But look closely, and you’ll see:

most of what we believe didn’t come from truth — <br>It came from **exposure**.

We live in a hall of mirrors, endlessly reflecting what we’ve consumed.<br>From our definition of success to our idea of love — <br>we’re often **repeating what we’ve seen**, not realizing we’re stuck in **someone else’s loop**.

It’s not your fault.<br>It’s just how **the game** is designed.

## The Factory You Forgot You Were Running

Imagine your mind as a **factory**.<br>Thoughts are the products.<br>Beliefs are the architecture.<br>Emotions are the signals from the quality control room.

Now ask yourself:<br>**What raw material have you been feeding into it?**

If all you consume is drama, gossip, noise, distraction…<br>don’t be surprised when your mind produces confusion, anxiety, and doubt.

- Input: comparison → Output: insecurity
- Input: fear-driven news → Output: tension
- Input: shallow opinions → Output: scattered identity

And yet, we scroll and sip it like morning coffee.

Most people are factory owners who’ve **abdicated their role**.<br>They let anything walk through the gates.<br>And then they blame the factory for its chaos.

## Begin Again With Awareness

Here’s a truth most avoid their whole lives:

**_You don’t have to believe everything you think._**

You don’t have to accept every idea that knocks at the door of your mind.<br>You are not your thoughts.<br>You are the one who **chooses** which ones to host, which ones to question, and which ones to burn.

That’s the quiet superpower.<br>You’re not a thinker by default.<br>You’re an **architect of thought.**

Every belief you hold is a **brick**.<br>Every word you absorb is a **seed**.<br>Every story you repeat is a **structure you inhabit**.

So the real question is not,

> <em>“What do I think?”<br> It’s:<br> “What have I allowed myself to absorb?”</em>

That’s where change begins.<br>Not in control, but in **curation**.

## You’ve Been Playing the Game Without Reading the Rules

Why do some people seem clearer, calmer, wiser?

Not because they were born with better brains.<br>But because they **guard the gates of their mind like a temple**.

They curate input like a chef curates ingredients.<br>They know that **junk thoughts** come from **junk consumption**.<br>And peace?<br>Peace is not an achievement. It’s a **byproduct of what you feed the mind**.

Want better thoughts?<br>Don’t force them.<br>**Feed better material** into the system.

Seek silence.<br>Read deeply.<br>Talk with people who stretch your perspective.<br>Watch things that awaken wonder instead of outrage.

Remember:<br>You can’t imagine a color you’ve never seen.<br>You can’t crave a kind of joy you’ve never tasted.<br>Your thoughts are shaped by **what’s already in the room**.

So maybe your restlessness isn’t a flaw.<br>Maybe it’s a signal that you’re hungry for something you’ve never consumed.

## From Consumption to Creation

You are what you eat — not just in body, but in mind.

Your internal world is not a mystery.<br>It is a mirror of everything you’ve allowed in.

So from this moment on, treat your attention like it matters.<br>Because it does.<br>Not in a productivity sense — but in a **who-you-become sense**.

Guard the gates.<br>Choose wisely.<br>Because every thought is a seed.<br>And your life is its flowering.

So the next time you think,

> _“Why am I like this?”_

First ask:

> _“What have I been feeding myself lately?”_

Because in the end, as the old saying goes:

> **“Yathā dṛśyate, tathā bhāvyate.”** (यथा दृश्यते, तथा भाव्यते)

mean

> **_As is seen, so it becomes._**

You don’t see reality.<br>You see reflections of what you’ve already absorbed.<br>Change what you consume — and everything changes with it.
